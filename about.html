Our Mission


At Beauty of Wild, our mission is to share the wonders of wildlife and nature with people all around the world. We believe in the power of nature to inspire, educate, and heal. Through our website, we aim to showcase the diverse animals and breathtaking landscapes found in places like the Garden Route National Park.

Who We Are


This project was started by Simon, a passionate wildlife enthusiast who loves exploring natural habitats and capturing their beauty through photography and storytelling. Whether you're a student, tourist, or nature lover, our goal is to help you connect with the wild.

What You’ll Find on Our Site


  • Information about harmless and dangerous animals
  • Photos of beautiful landscapes
  • Links to wildlife documentaries and videos
  • Educational content about nature and ecosystems
